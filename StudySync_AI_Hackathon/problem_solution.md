# Problem Statement & Solution

## Problem Statement
Develop an application that helps students plan and track their studies based on subjects, available study time, examinations, assignments, and progress.

## Solution — StudySync AI
A personalized and adaptive study management platform.

### Intelligent Priority Formula
Each topic receives a dynamic score based on:
- Deadline urgency
- Difficulty
- Remaining progress
- Whether the task was missed

Higher score = higher priority.

### Adaptive Scheduling
The scheduler fills the student's available hours with the highest-priority unfinished topics. If a task is missed, the system recalculates the remaining plan instead of leaving the student with an outdated timetable.

### Innovation
The system creates a feedback loop:

Student data → Priority engine → Study plan → Student progress → Reschedule → Updated plan

### Impact
- Reduces planning effort
- Prevents forgotten deadlines
- Focuses time on weaker subjects
- Makes recovery from missed study sessions easier
- Gives students a clear view of progress
